05 — FAQ

Quick answers.

How long does a project take?

Most websites ship in 10–14 days. AI chatbots in 5–7 days. AI calling agents in 7–10 days depending on integrations. Video clone pipelines take 7 days to set up, then run on retainer.

Do you work with clients outside Tezpur / Assam?

Yes — we work with clients across India and globally. Everything runs over WhatsApp, Google Meet, and a private project dashboard. Location is irrelevant.

What does the AI calling agent actually do?

It picks up your business calls, qualifies leads, books appointments, and follows up — in a natural human-sounding voice. It can also make outbound calls (cold outreach, reminders, reactivation campaigns) and hand off to a human when needed.

Is video cloning ethical / safe?

We only clone the creator's own face and voice with full written consent. The output is used to scale your own content, never to impersonate anyone else. We refuse projects that involve cloning a third party.

What do you need from me to get started?

A 30-min discovery call and a 50% advance to lock the slot. Everything else — content, brand assets, integrations — we collect on a structured doc after kickoff.

Do you offer maintenance after launch?

Yes. Every project includes 30 days of free post-launch support. After that, optional retainers start at ₹8,000/month for websites and ₹15,000/month for AI agents (covers updates, monitoring, and improvements).
